HEARTWOOD — grow the last grove through four seasons. A grove-defense game: pests march from the right across five lanes, and you plant Thornspikes, Sunpetals, Brambles, Sporecups and Frostferns, harvest pollen, pick a graft between seasons, and hold the Heart Tree for 12 waves across Spring, Summer, Autumn and Winter. Survive Winter and the grove blooms.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — no build step, no network, just static files under `game/`.

## How to play

- **Mouse:** pick a seed from the bar, click soil to plant. Right-click digs a plant up (half refund).
- **Keys 1–5:** pick a seed. **Space:** start the next wave (early start pays bonus pollen). **P:** pause. **M:** mute. **R:** restart.
- **Goal:** keep the Heart Tree alive through 12 waves. Between seasons, choose one graft — Deep Roots, Sharp Thorns, Golden Pollen, Swift Sap, Frostbite, or Second Wind.
- **Plants:** Thornspike shoots down its lane · Sunpetal grows pollen · Bramble blocks (flying pests ignore it) · Sporecap lobs splash damage · Frostfern chills everything nearby.
- **Pests:** Aphids, Beetles, Wasps, Dusk Moths, armored Snails — and the Blight Stag in the final wave.
